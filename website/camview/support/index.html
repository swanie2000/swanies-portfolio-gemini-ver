Support

Help for the Android camera app and the Windows helper on your Blue Iris PC.

What you need

  • Blue Iris V6 on your recording PC.
  • The Android app on the phone.
  • CamView Push on that same PC, so the phone can pair and get motion alerts.

The cameras stay on your Blue Iris server. This app is not a second cloud copy of the video.

Downloads

The Android app and the Windows setup are listed on the Camera Viewer page. They are not on Google Play.
