What you download

GPS BOX is not on Google Play. When it ships, you will download the Android app here, plus a Raspberry Pi image you write to an SD card.

Home Wi‑Fi is how phones talk to the box when you are in the house. When you are out, a small yearly pipe lets those phones find your Pi. We do not store your trips. If that pipe lapses, we still do not hold your history — the hardware is yours.

A ready-made box

I may also offer a Raspberry Pi in a case, with a power supply, already set up to run GPS BOX — for people who would rather plug it in than flash an SD card.

That kit is not locked in yet. If you want one, say so on the contact form. Interest will help decide whether it becomes a real product.

Frequently asked questions.

Quick answers about Swanie's GPS BOX.

How is this different from Life360?

Life360’s cloud is the product. GPS BOX’s product is a box in your house. Phones update your Pi. The family sees the map. A tracking company does not keep a file on every trip to school, the gym, and work.

Will this be on Google Play?

No. Like Swanie's Camera Viewer, GPS BOX is sideloaded from this site — the Android APK and the Pi image, when they are ready.

What hardware do I need?

Android phones for the family, and a Raspberry Pi at home with an SD card you flash from the image on this page. Optionally, a ready-made Pi in a case with power — if I offer it.

Where is location data stored?

On your family’s Pi. A meeting point only helps phones away from home reach that box. It is a pipe, not a second map database.
