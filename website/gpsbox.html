The family map stays at home

Install the Android app from this page, plug in the GPS BOX, and tap Find GPS BOX on house Wi-Fi. The first phone is the manager. Later phones pick a name and join. GPS BOX does not ask a member for an email.

Android app

Install it on each family phone from the file on this page. The map, saved places, check-in, and the last 30 days are in the app.

The GPS BOX

Write the image to a memory card, or use a ready-made GPS BOX. Plug the Ethernet cable into the router, wait 2 minutes, then Find it from the app.

One plan

The manager's email is billed for the family, up to 8 people. That address is not shown on the map. If the plan lapses, the map at home still works. Away-from-home updates need an active plan.

Frequently asked questions.

Quick answers about Swanie's GPS BOX.

How is this different from Life360?

Life360 keeps the map. GPS BOX keeps the map on a box in your house. The family sees it. A tracking company does not keep a file of every trip.

Will this be on Google Play?

No. You install the Android app from the file on this page.

What hardware do I need?

Android phones, and a GPS BOX at home. Write the image to a memory card yourself, or use a ready-made GPS BOX. That ready-made box is not for sale on this page yet.

Where is location data stored?

On your GPS BOX at home. Phones in the house reach it on your Wi-Fi. Phones away from home reach that same box through the monthly or yearly plan. We do not keep a second copy of the map.

Who is billed?

One plan per GPS BOX, on the manager's email, for up to 8 people. Members pick a name. GPS BOX does not ask a member for an email. That email is not shown on the map.
