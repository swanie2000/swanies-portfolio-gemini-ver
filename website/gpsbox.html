Frequently asked questions.

Quick answers about Swanie's GPS BOX.

How is this different from Life360?

Life360 keeps the map. GPS BOX keeps the map on a box in your house. The family sees it. A tracking company does not keep a file of every trip.

Will this be on Google Play?

No. You install the Android app from the file on this page.

What hardware do I need?

Android phones, and a GPS BOX at home. Write the image to a memory card yourself, or use a ready-made GPS BOX. That ready-made box is not for sale on this page yet.

Where is location data stored?

On your GPS BOX at home. Phones in the house reach it on your Wi-Fi. Phones away from home reach that same box through the monthly or yearly plan. We do not keep a second copy of the map.

Who is billed?

One plan per GPS BOX, on the manager's email, for up to 8 people. $79 a year, or $8.99 a month. Members pick a name. GPS BOX does not ask a member for an email. That email is not shown on the map.
